# Design System

## Tokens

- **Primary accent:** Cyan `#01FFFF`
- **Background:** Abyss black `#010A0A`
- **Heading font:** Bebas Neue (Google Fonts)
- **Body/code font:** JetBrains Mono (Google Fonts)

## Aesthetic

Dark, neon, underwater/shark theme. Key motifs:
- "Teeth" SVG dividers between sections
- Caustic water Canvas animation in the background
- Spotlight beam in the hero
- Glow and neon effects on text and cards

## Tone & Copy

- Bold and direct — no corporate softness
- Web3-native language is fine (degen, rugged, ATH, on-chain, etc.)
- Emojis in easter egg / notification copy only — never in main UI text
- The shark/JAWS metaphor should feel cool, not gimmicky
